***My Favorite Series: Stranger Things***
**About the Series**
Stranger Things is my favorite science fiction and horror series on Netflix. It was created by the Duffer Brothers and is set in a small town called Hawkins, Indiana. The story begins when a young boy named Will Byers mysteriously disappears. His friends discover a strange girl named Eleven and uncover secrets about a dangerous place called the Upside Down.

**My Favorite Characters**
**Eleven****Mike Wheeler****Dustin Henderson****Steve Harrington****Max Mayfield**

**Why I Love This Series**
I love this series because it has exciting and mysterious scenes. Interesting and lovable characters especially their amazing friendships and teamwork. I also love the unexpected twists and scary moments and its great combination of adventure, horror, and science fiction.
